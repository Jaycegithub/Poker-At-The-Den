## Issues discovered (need client decision)

1. **Address conflict.** Home page says *6320 Dalrock Rd, Suite 100*; Contact Us and Places To Eat say *6230 Dalrock Rd*. The Rowlett Area Chamber lists 6320 Dalrock Rd, Suite 100, and Google Maps places 6320 directly across from Dalrock Diner (matching the Places To Eat copy). **6230 is very likely a typo.** Text has been kept verbatim; the map uses 6320.
2. **Broken links** — "Become A Member" (home hero), "Join the Club" (About, FAQs), "Become a Member" (Why Join) and "Membership Info" (Tournaments) point to `/membershhip/` (typo, 404). Redesign links them to `/membership/`.
3. **PokerAtlas** menu item links to `#`; no PokerAtlas URL exists anywhere on the site and no public listing was found. Shown as "Link pending" until the URL is supplied.
4. **Celebrity look-alike image.** `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-3.webp` (Why Join background) is AI-generated and its central figure closely resembles a well-known actor. It could imply an endorsement. **Not used in the redesign**; recommend removing it from the live site.
5. **AI-generated imagery.** Most "lounge" and player images (`The-Den-Poker-Lounge-*`, `Gemini_Generated_Image_*`) appear AI-generated (stylised, "THE DEN" printed on felt). They are reused as the site already uses them, without captions claiming they are photos of the club. Replacing them with real photography of the room is the single biggest credibility upgrade available.
6. **Two Facebook URLs.** Site footer links `facebook.com/TheDenPokerLounge`; the brief references `facebook.com/thedentx`. Both redirect to a login wall, so neither could be reviewed. Confirm which is current.
7. **Weak meta descriptions** generated by Rank Math from page fragments: Poker/Chess/Dominoes/Spades/Rowlett Community = "01", Explore Rowlett = "Boating", Places To Eat = "Burgers", Private Gatherings = "Televisions", Community Events = "Morning", Play Poker Cash = "Nothing yet.", Founders = an error message. Kept verbatim (SEO preservation rule) — recommend rewriting.
8. **Rank Math entity** is configured as Person/Organization named "thedenpokerlounge.com" (domain instead of business name). Kept as-is; recommend fixing and adding `LocalBusiness` schema once the address is confirmed.
9. **Leftover template posts.** The 3 blog posts date from June 2025, one slug still references "ace-high"; two have no body content. Consider unpublishing.
10. **Copy typos kept verbatim:** "Comfort for the long game.." (double period), "monthly Member" (lower-case), "Trust the room and Transparent.", chip label "Increasing" on Poker page (likely "Increasing Blinds").
11. **Logo** has black lettering on transparency (designed for light backgrounds). A light-on-dark variant was derived from the original file for the dark redesign; the original is kept for light uses.
12. `/resources/` exists as an empty published page (menu "Resources" is `#`).
