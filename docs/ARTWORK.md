# Homepage imagery

The homepage mixes two kinds of imagery and uses no third-party photos.

## The club's own photos
These are reused from the current pokerattheden.com uploads:

- `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-7`: hero option B (arch photo)
- `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-11`, `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-8` and `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-3-1`: the club / people / seat photo stage
- `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-6`: Poker tile
- `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-9-1` and `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-7-1`: social section
- `Tournaments-Hero-bg`: poker band
- `The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-9`: business banner

`The-Den-Poker-Lounge-Image-3` is deliberately left out (see AUDIT.md, item 4).

## Original SVG illustrations
These are generated by `tools/make_art.py` into `src/assets/img/art/`. They're built from simple geometry in the site palette and have no external sources.

| File | Used for | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| `art-hand.svg` | Not currently used | A royal hand (A♠ K♥ Q♦ J♣) fanned above chip stacks |
| `art-seat.svg` | Not currently used | A table from above under a lamp, with one gold seat marked Reserved |
| `art-chess.svg` | Chess tile | Rook, king, knight and pawn on the edge of a board |
| `art-dominoes.svg` | Dominoes tile | A double-six layout with the double laid crosswise, plus the boneyard |
| `art-chips.svg` | Hero options D and F | Three chip stacks on a transparent background |
| `art-dealer.svg` | Hero options D and F | The dealer button |
| `art-spades.svg` | Spades tile | A hand of spades from 10 to A, with a bid marker |

To change one, edit `tools/make_art.py` and run `python3 tools/make_art.py`.

## Hero options
The homepage carries six hero options while the design is in review:

- A: a fan of real cards built in CSS
- B: arch photo
- C: the flop, turn and river
- D: the hero is a poker table seen from above
- E: a club photo with a TV-broadcast poker overlay
- F: pocket aces revealed on the felt

Option A shows by default; the others are rendered `hidden`. Once one is chosen, delete the rest from `src/templates/pages/home.html` and `src/templates/partials/cards.html`.
